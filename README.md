# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 30, 2026

Welcome to **Study Station** ("the App").

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App stores, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

---

# Summary

Your data belongs to you.

Most of your information is stored **only on your device**. If you choose to enable **Family Sync**, your data is securely synchronized using **Google Firebase** so it can be shared only among your family's devices.

We **do not sell**, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or other commercial organizations.

---

# Information Stored on Your Device

The App may store the following information locally on your device:

* Your name and education level
* Study schedules and timetables
* Tasks and reminders
* Flashcards and study materials
* Progress, achievements, points, and statistics
* Faith and worship tracking data
* App settings and preferences

This information remains on your device unless you choose to enable Family Sync.

---

# Family Cloud Sync (Optional)

Family Sync is completely optional.

If enabled, the information listed above is securely synchronized using **Google Firebase** so that it can be shared between authorized devices belonging to your family.

Access to your family's data is protected by a **Family Code** that you create.

**Please keep your Family Code private**, as anyone with this code may be able to access your family's synchronized data.

---

# Advertising

The App may display advertisements through **Google AdMob**.

To provide a safer experience for students and families, the App is configured to use:

* Family-friendly advertisements only
* Google "Designed for Families" settings where applicable
* Child-directed treatment settings where appropriate
* No personalized advertising based on behavioral tracking

Google may collect limited anonymous information as described in Google's own Privacy Policy.

---

# Information We Do NOT Collect

The App does **not** intentionally collect:

* User account registrations
* Email addresses
* Phone numbers
* Device location
* Contact lists
* Photos
* Camera content
* Microphone recordings

unless you explicitly grant permission for a specific feature that requires it.

---

# Children's Privacy

Study Station is designed for students and may be used by children under the supervision of a parent or guardian.

The App does not provide communication with strangers or public user-generated content.

Parents or guardians maintain control of family data through the private Family Code.

We are committed to protecting children's privacy in accordance with applicable laws and Google Play Families policies.

---

# Your Rights

You can manage your data at any time.

### Delete Local Data

Settings → Clear Data → Full Reset

### Disable Family Sync

Family Sync → Disconnect

### Export Your Data

Family Dashboard → Backup

### Delete Cloud Data

You may request permanent deletion of your synchronized cloud data by contacting the developer.

Cloud data deletion requests will be processed within **30 days**.

---

# Data Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect your information.

Although no method of electronic storage or Internet transmission is completely secure, we continuously work to protect your data against unauthorized access.
